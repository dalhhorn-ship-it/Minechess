# Minechess Versions

## v0.1 MVP: "Can a kid play a fun game against a creature?"

**Goal:** prove the core loop. A child picks a creature, plays a full game, and the creature feels alive.

**Features:** F01, F02, F03, F04, F06, F07, F08, F12, F23, with 3 creatures: Wobble (L1), Copper Bot (L5), Deep Watcher (L8). One default profile, all 3 unlocked. Placeholder parent gate for language.

**Definition of done:**
1. A full legal game can be played to checkmate, stalemate or a draw in iPad Safari.
2. The three creatures play at clearly different strengths and react with all emotions from PRD section 6.
3. Everything is available in Dutch and English.
4. Both kids (5 and 9) have played it, and the owner has noted their reactions.

## v1.0: Full first release for the family

**Goal:** every child has their own profile and a ladder to climb, and the game teaches.

**Features:** all Must and Should features: F01 to F18 and F23, all 8 creatures.

**Definition of done:**
1. All acceptance criteria for v0.1 and v1.0 pass.
2. Creature tuning checked: the 5 year old wins most games against Level 1 and 2; the 9 year old finds Level 8 hard.
3. Parent gate tested: no child in the family can get past it unaided.
4. Every screen passes the `ux-ipad-kids` review checklist.

## v2.0: Mine chess and more

**Goal:** new ways to play once classic chess is familiar.

**Features:** F19 mine chess, F20 two players on one iPad, F21 parent progress view, F22 profile backup, more puzzles.

**Definition of done:** set when v2.0 is planned. Mine chess rules need their own mini PRD first.
